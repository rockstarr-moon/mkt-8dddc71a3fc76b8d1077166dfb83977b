Quickstart

Rockstarr Outreach, Sales Navigator Edition

Before your first session

  • A Sales Navigator seat, signed in in your default browser.
  • Your baseline ICP (from the onboarding workbook). We refine it per-campaign in the session.
  • A saved search in Sales Nav matching the first campaign's ICP.
  • 45 minutes on the calendar with your Rockstarr lead.
  • A booking link (Calendly, GrowthAmp, etc.) OR access to share your Google Calendar.

How you talk to the system

You type short, plain-English instructions into a Cowork task. No commands, no menus. The system asks follow-up questions when it needs to and shows you drafts before anything leaves your workspace. You do not need to know skill names — “Run today's outreach loop” and “Let's do today's LinkedIn run” both work.

Your first campaign, end to end

  1. In a new task, type: “Draft a new outreach campaign.” The first question is the campaign type — full-sequence (connect + three-message conversation) or connect-only (connection requests only, no follow-up sequence). Pick whichever fits the goal.

  2. Answer the rest of the questions one at a time: role, company size, geography, disqualifiers. For full-sequence, also: the single pain this ICP feels and an anchor phrase naming it in your voice. (Connect-only skips those last two by design.) Plain English. If you are not sure, say “not sure — propose something.”

  3. Review the drafted four-message sequence. Push back if anything is off: “Message 2 should open on [specific observation], not a generic question.” “Message 3 is too long — cut it in half.” Repeat until it reads like you.

  4. Type: “Approve this campaign.”

  5. Type: “Register the campaign and crawl the saved search.” Have the saved-search URL from Sales Nav ready. The system pulls leads into your tracker and seeds connect tasks. The campaign goes live in the next daily run.

Your daily rhythm (10 min)

  • Morning (2 min). Open Cowork — or open the urgent reply email if one landed overnight. Type: “Run today's outreach loop.” The system confirms your LinkedIn session, sends the day's connect requests up to the cap, detects accepts, schedules and sends sequence messages, pulls in new replies, and reports back a summary.

  • Midday (5 to 7 min). When the urgent reply email lands (or whenever you check), type: “Show me the replies waiting for my approval.” For each draft, respond with “Approve,” “Edit: [your changes],” “Reject. Reason: [short note],” or “Not interested. Opt them out.”

  • That's it. Close the task.

If you want to eyeball the day before anything ships, type “Just preview today's queue before we send” instead. The system shows you who is about to get a connect and where each active lead sits. When you are satisfied, type “Go ahead.”

Other things you will need to type

  • Pause or stop a campaign: “Pause the [campaign] campaign.” / “Stop the [campaign] campaign.”

  • Manual booking: “Mark the meeting with [name] as booked for [time].”

  • Friday report: “Generate this week's outreach report.”

  • Weekly backup: “Back up the outreach workbook.”

  • Any question: Describe what you want. “Why did my reply rate drop this week?” and “Show me the last three messages sent to [lead].” both work.

Your Friday rhythm (30 min)

  • Open this week's report in 06_reports/weekly/.

  • Review the summary and per-campaign numbers with your Rockstarr lead.

  • Approve any adjustments for next week: tighten an ICP, pause a campaign, add one.

If something goes sideways

  • LinkedIn warning. Call your Rockstarr lead immediately. We pause everything.

  • Reply queue is overwhelming. Tell your Rockstarr lead. We slow the connect rate.

  • Meeting booked but not on your calendar. Tell your Rockstarr lead. We reconcile.

  • Anything else. Call your Rockstarr lead.

Your safety net

  • 20 connection requests per day, 100 per week, enforced in code.
  • Nothing sends without your approval for replies and new campaigns.
  • Every run verifies the correct LinkedIn account before anything goes out.
  • Every draft passes a voice pass and an AI-slop scrub before it hits your queue.
  • Sequence messages personalize per lead at send time, with a clean fallback so a missing name never ships as a placeholder.
  • Same-run urgent emails when replies land — draft response inline, click-through into your reply queue. The email only fires when something new is waiting.
  • Every week, you see every number, including the ones that went the wrong way.
